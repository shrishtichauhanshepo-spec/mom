Dear Mom,

I don't say it enough, but you mean more to me than words can hold. In the middle of everything, the busy days and the quiet nights, it's always you on my mind. You were the one who noticed when something was off before I said a word, and the one who asked if I had eaten even when I was far too old for that.

I didn't always see how much you carried so that everything around me felt easy. I see it now. A lot of who I am started with you: the way you showed me what kindness looks like without ever making a speech about it.

Today belongs to you. No chores, no worrying about anyone else. Bake the pretend cake, spin the wheel, scratch the cards, and let me spoil you a little, because you've spent years doing it for me.

Happy birthday, Mom. If I had to choose again, I'd still pick you, in every version of this life, every single time.

With all my love, Shrishti
